# Slide Annotation

Generate detailed study notes for the following slide from a presentation.

## Slide {slide_number}

### Text on the Slide

```
{slide_text}
```

### Speaker's Narration (Transcript)

**Time Range**: {start_time} – {end_time}

```
{transcript_text}
```

## Instructions

Create comprehensive notes that combine the information shown on the slide with what the speaker said. The notes should capture everything important for someone studying this material.

Focus on:

- **Key concepts** presented on the slide and elaborated by the speaker
- **Definitions** of technical terms
- **Important facts**, numbers, specifications, or configurations mentioned
- **Practical examples** or demonstrations the speaker described
- **Context and explanations** the speaker provided beyond what's on the slide
- **Relationships** between concepts on this slide and the broader topic

## Output Format

### Key Points

- [Main concepts and takeaways as bullet points]

### Details

[Detailed notes expanding on the slide content with information from the speaker's narration. Use sub-bullets, bold terms, and clear formatting.]

### Definitions

- **[Term]**: [Definition as explained by the speaker]

### Examples

- [Any concrete examples, demonstrations, or scenarios mentioned]

### Key Takeaways 🎯

- [Most important things to remember from this slide]

---

IMPORTANT:

- Only include content that was actually on the slide or mentioned in the transcript
- Do not invent information not present in either source
- If the transcript is sparse, focus on the slide text content
- If the slide text is minimal, focus on the speaker's narration
- Combine both sources for the richest possible notes
